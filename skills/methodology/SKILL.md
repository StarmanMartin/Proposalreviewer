# Evaluating the methodology

Judge whether the planned work can actually answer the research question.

Check:
1. **Fit** – Do the chosen methods/techniques match the question? Is it explained why
   these methods (and not others) are used?
2. **Work plan** – Are the steps, samples, measurements or simulations described concretely
   (what, how many, in which order)?
3. **Controls and validation** – Are reference samples, controls, statistics, error
   estimates or cross-checks planned?
4. **Data** – Is it stated how data will be analysed, and how results will be interpreted?
5. **Risks** – Are critical steps identified, with a fallback if they fail?

Scoring guide:
- Top: concrete, well-justified plan with controls and risk mitigation.
- Middle: plausible plan with gaps (missing controls, unclear sample numbers).
- Bottom: methods do not address the question or are not described.
