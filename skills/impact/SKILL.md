# Evaluating expected impact

Judge what will come out of the project and who benefits.

Check:
1. **Outcomes** – Are expected results and deliverables stated concretely (publications,
   datasets, prototypes, follow-up projects)?
2. **Dissemination** – Is it planned how results are published and data made available
   (e.g. FAIR data, open access)?
3. **Broader impact** – Relevance for applications, industry, society, education or
   early-career researchers.
4. **Follow-up** – Does the project enable further work (larger grant, collaboration)?

Scoring guide:
- Top: clear, credible outcomes with a dissemination and data plan.
- Middle: outcomes plausible but vaguely described.
- Bottom: no identifiable outcome or benefit.
