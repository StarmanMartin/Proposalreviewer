# Evaluating feasibility

Judge whether the proposal is realistic.

Check:
1. **Resources** – Are the requested instruments, facility time, materials or compute
   quantified? Is the amount justified by the work plan (e.g. hours per sample × samples)?
2. **Time line** – Is the schedule realistic, including sample preparation and analysis?
3. **Team** – Does the team have (or have access to) the needed expertise? Is prior
   experience with the techniques shown?
4. **Preparedness** – Are samples/preliminary results available, or is there a credible
   path to them?
5. **Safety and ethics** – Are hazardous materials, safety measures or regulatory
   requirements mentioned where relevant?

Scoring guide:
- Top: resources precisely justified, team experienced, preliminary data available.
- Middle: feasible but with unquantified or weakly justified requests.
- Bottom: requests clearly over/under-sized or key capabilities missing.
