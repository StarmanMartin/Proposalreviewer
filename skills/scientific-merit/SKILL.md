# Evaluating scientific merit

Judge whether the research question is worth answering and clearly stated.

Check:
1. **Research question** – Is there a clear, specific question or hypothesis? Vague goals
   ("study the properties of X") score low.
2. **State of the art** – Does the proposal position itself against existing work and cite
   relevant literature? Does it explain what is not known yet?
3. **Novelty** – What is new: the material, the method, the combination, the scale?
   Incremental work is acceptable but must be named as such.
4. **Relevance** – Is the expected knowledge gain significant for the field or for
   applications? Is it explained why now?

Scoring guide:
- Top of scale: sharp hypothesis, clear gap in the literature, significant expected advance.
- Middle: sound but incremental or with a loosely defined question.
- Bottom: no identifiable question, no context, or already solved problem.
